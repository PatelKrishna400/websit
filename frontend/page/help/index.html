❓ Frequently Asked Questions

🎫 How does the 500-Level Battle Pass work? ▼
Every completed task advances you 1 Pass Level toward Level 500. Both Free Track rewards and Pass Track (3,000 ⭐) unlock Tickets, Coins, and Stars automatically as you level up.
⚡ What are Ultra Credits & Gift Cards? ▼
Completing Ultra Tasks grants Ultra Credits used in the Wallet alongside Stars to redeem the Time Offer and high-value Amazon Gift Card milestones.
⏳ Why did my Task Verify button stay locked? ▼
Tap ▶ Open first and let the countdown reach zero. Once finished, ✓ Verify unlocks automatically so you can submit the 6-digit code.
👥 How do Invite Friends Star rewards credit? ▼
Share your unique referral code from Profile → Invite Friends. When friends redeem your code, your active milestone (1 Friend → +10 ⭐, 5 Friends → +20 ⭐, etc.) becomes claimable inside the popup!
